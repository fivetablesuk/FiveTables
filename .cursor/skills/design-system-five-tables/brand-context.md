## Layout Rules

- Minimal, symmetrical, spacious, polished
- Thin gold rules / framed sections
- Generous whitespace; centred compositions where appropriate
- Outlined + solid gold bordered buttons (no rounded-full pills)
- Avoid cards-as-decoration, bright colours, icon grids, social feeds, corporate stock

## Imagery

Use: elegant hospitality interiors, moody lounges, warm ambient light, refined evening settings.  
Avoid: handshakes, corporate teams, cheesy networking photos.

## Anti-feel

Must not feel like LinkedIn, a networking org, a conference site, a startup landing page, a social feed, or a corporate directory.
