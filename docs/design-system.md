# Five Tables — Design System

## Brand

- **Name:** Five Tables
- **Tagline:** Good Company. Exceptional Conversation. / Five Sectors. One Community.
- **Positioning:** Invitation-led private community for Black professionals in London — not networking, not transactional.
- **Mood:** Elegant, exclusive, intimate, warm, editorial, timeless, premium, understated.

## Colour palette

| Role | Value | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Background | `#000000` | Full-bleed dark surfaces |
| Ivory / cream text | warm off-white | Primary readable type on dark |
| Muted / champagne gold | `#C5A059` | Accents, rules, CTAs, labels |
| Soft taupe / stone | subtle neutrals | Secondary text and dividers |

## Typography

| Use | Font |
|-----|------|
| Wordmark, display, section headlines | Cormorant Garamond |
| Labels, body, buttons | Jost |

- Weights: 300 · 400 · 500
- Refined letter-spacing on caps and labels
- Sharp corners (no border radius) unless a later screen requires otherwise

## The Five Tables

| Numeral | Sector |
|---------|--------|
| I | Medicine & Dentistry |
| II | Law & Legal Services |
| III | Banking, Finance & FinTech |
| IV | Technology & Engineering |
| V | Business & Leadership |

## Layout principles

- Minimal, symmetrical, spacious, polished
- Thin gold rules and generous whitespace
- Outlined and solid gold bordered buttons
- Avoid decorative cards, bright colours, icon grids, and corporate networking visuals

## Imagery

Use: elegant hospitality interiors, moody lounges, warm ambient light, refined evening settings.  
Avoid: handshakes, corporate teams, stock networking photography.

## Anti-patterns

The product must not feel like LinkedIn, a networking organisation, a conference site, a startup landing page, or a corporate directory.
